Established on May 19, 2020, Uday Pratap Pharmacy & Law College was created to bridge high-standard pharmaceutical science and comprehensive legal education with regional accessibility. Nurturing over 500+ active scholars across professional Pharmacy and Law degree pathways.

Our Infrastructure

Modern Labs & Facilities

Hi-Tech Computer Labs

State-of-the-art digital infrastructure built to support contemporary technical learning.

  • High-configuration computing terminals loaded with research software and analytical tools.
  • Campus-wide high-speed Wi-Fi and dedicated gigabit internet backbone for uninterrupted research.
  • Integrated access to digital repositories, legal portals, and scientific journal databases.

Advanced Pharmacy Labs

Specialized practical environments tailored for pharmaceutical research and experimentation.

  • PCI-compliant laboratories for Pharmaceutics, Pharmaceutical Chemistry, and Pharmacology.
  • High-precision testing apparatus and analytical instruments for hands-on experimentation.
  • Dedicated Quality Assurance and Pharmacognosy facilities equipped for modern drug formulation studies.

Computerized Central Library

An extensive knowledge hub supporting academic inquiry across all faculties.

  • Automated library management system with barcode indexing and digital catalog search.
  • Rich collection of reference textbooks, national/international journals, and legal law reporters.
  • Quiet reading spaces and e-learning terminals for scholarly research and self-study.
