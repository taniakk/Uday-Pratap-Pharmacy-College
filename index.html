About Uday Pratap Pharmacy College

Building the Next Era of Healthcare Leaders

Located along the Sakaldiha-Saidpur corridor, Uday Pratap Pharmacy College combines cutting-edge clinical infrastructure, industry-driven pedagogy, and holistic research to shape skilled medical professionals.

A-Grade Infrastructure UP-East Region

State-of-the-Art Chemical & Clinical Suites

Transforming Academic Rigor into Practical Expertise

Established on May 19, 2020, Uday Pratap Pharmacy College was created to bridge high-standard pharmaceutical science with regional accessibility. Today, our institution nurtures over 300+ active scholars across specialized Diploma and Degree pathways.

Through real-world formulation labs, industrial industrial tie-ups, and continuous hospital exposure, we prepare students to lead in retail pharmacy, quality assurance, regulatory compliance, and medical research.

Our Facilities

Modern Labs & Infrastructure

Hi-Tech Computer Labs

Uday Pratap Group of Institutions provides state-of-the-art IT infrastructure for all students.

  • Advanced computer labs equipped with modern high-configuration systems.
  • High-speed Wi-Fi enabled campus network with dedicated 100Mbps connectivity.
  • Comprehensive digital resources supporting academic research and practical coursework.

Pharmacy Labs & Moot Court

Equipped with specialized domain-specific facilities for practical learning.

  • PCI-compliant Pharmaceutical Chemistry, Pharmaceutics, and Pharmacology laboratories.
  • Dedicated Moot Court hall designed for legal simulation and judicial training for Law students.
  • Modern instruments and testing equipment for practical experimentation and research.

Computerized Central Library

The academic heart of Uday Pratap Group of Institutions.

  • Fully computerized library system equipped with barcode management and digital cataloging.
  • Extensive repository of over 40,000 books, national/international journals, and legal reporters.
  • Spacious, quiet reading zones and digital access to online research databases for students and faculty.
