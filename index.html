Transforming Passion into Healthcare Leadership

Founded on May 19, 2020, Uday Pratap Pharmacy College was created to bridge high-standard medical education with accessibility. Today, our campus houses over 300+ thriving pharmacy students across D.Pharm and B.Pharm degree programs.

Through rigorous lab work, real-world industrial exposure, and state-of-the-art facilities, we prepare our scholars to excel in retail pharmacy, clinical research, quality control, and pharmaceutical manufacturing.

Diploma

D.Pharm (Diploma in Pharmacy)

2 Years Full-Time Course

  • Eligibility: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Maths
  • Focus: Retail & Community Pharmacy, Hospital Training
  • Outcome: Registered Pharmacist license upon completion

Undergraduate

B.Pharm (Bachelor of Pharmacy)

4 Years Full-Time Degree

  • Eligibility: 10+2 with PCB/PCM with qualifying percentage
